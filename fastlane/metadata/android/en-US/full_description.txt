Treso: follow your money month by month, what comes in, what goes out and what's left.

MADE FOR YOUR LIFE
On first launch, a few questions are enough: where your money comes from, what you pay every month, what you spend on day to day. Treso then only suggests what applies to you. No rent? No rent step.

THE WHOLE MONTH AT A GLANCE
• Current balance, what this month added and the expected end-of-month balance
• Spending by category and an optional monthly budget
• Quick add for everyday expenses, in one tap

YOUR RECURRING ITEMS
Salary around the 26th, rent by the 5th, bills by the 3rd: Treso lists them every month under "Upcoming" with their date, and you confirm with one tap once it's paid or received.

SEVERAL CURRENCIES
CFA franc, Guinean franc and US dollar. Each amount keeps the currency it was entered in; switching currency converts at the day's rate, or at your own.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No account, no server, no ads, no tracking
• Everything stays on your phone
• Full backup to import on another phone, and spreadsheet export (CSV) for Excel or Google Sheets

In French and English, following your phone's language.
